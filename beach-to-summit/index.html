The island you inherited from your grandmother has been deserted since a storm years ago. Ferry captain Deniz drops you ashore, and your mission is clear: turn it into the finest holiday paradise on the seas!

🏝️ BUILD YOUR ISLAND FROM SCRATCH

Start with a reception, then open guesthouses, hotels, restaurants, cafés and shops. Aquapark, beach club, funfair, go-karts, zoo, golf, marina… over 140 facilities are waiting.

🏟️ NEW: SPORTS ISLAND

A giant stadium, horse racing, an Olympic pool, an athletics track, a BMX park, paragliding and concerts at the amphitheater! Bring athletes and fans to your island and host the Island Games.

👷 HIRE YOUR STAFF

Every facility runs on its own team. Hire Novice, Skilled and Master staff, pay their wages and multiply your earnings.

🧳 KEEP YOUR GUESTS HAPPY

Families, honeymooners, adventurers, athletes, sports fans… Every group has its own wishes. Meet them, earn stars and raise your reputation.

🗺️ EXPLORE 8 ZONES

From the beach and the town to the luxury hilltop, the nature zone and the new sports zones: unlock the zones behind the fog. Bring more guests by ferry, airport, helipad and cruise port.

⭐ A LIVING ISLAND

Watch the Ferris wheel turn, horses race, the crowd do the wave and pools fill with swimmers. As you upgrade, your island comes alive before your eyes.

💰 EARN, UPGRADE, GROW

Collect the money your facilities earn, upgrade buildings up to three levels and complete Captain Deniz’s quests. Your island keeps earning even while you’re offline — idle earnings for up to 8 hours.

📶 PLAY OFFLINE

No Wi-Fi? No problem: build and grow your island without an internet connection. (The leaderboard, weekly race, cloud save and purchases need a connection.)

☁️ YOUR PROGRESS IS SAFE

Google Play Games saves your progress to the cloud, so you can continue right where you left off on a new phone.

Love island games, hotel and resort tycoon games or business simulators? From the beach to the summit — you’re the island tycoon!
